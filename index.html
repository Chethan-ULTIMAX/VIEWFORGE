What makes a good input?

Use matching orthographic views of the same object. Keep the whole object visible, centred, and clearly separated from a plain background.

ⓘ
Know what visual hull can recover

This method estimates the outer volume. Hidden concavities, internal details, materials and textures cannot be recovered from silhouettes alone.
